Only pickup timestamps in 2024 enter the charts. Counts retain records with questionable duration, distance or fare; each metric uses its own eligible denominator. Unavailable fields remain unavailable.

Metric definitions and scope

Duration: dropoff minus pickup, greater than zero and no more than four hours. Source timestamps are timezone-naive NYC local clock values; no UTC conversion or DST correction is inferred.

Distance: reported miles greater than zero and at most 200. FHV does not report distance. Speed: total eligible miles ÷ total eligible duration, restricted to valid duration/distance pairs with implied speed at most 100 mph. It is not a straight-line estimate.

Fares: Yellow/Green metered fare and high-volume base passenger fare use different definitions. No combined fare total is shown. Nonnegative base-fare values up to $1,000 are included in service-specific averages. FHV fares are unavailable.

Quality: flags are overlapping and are shown for the selected source-file partition month and service. Projected duplicate candidates are identical selected analytical fields within one processing batch; they are not proven duplicate trips and are preserved. No person or vehicle identity is inferred.

Source populations: TLC publishes Yellow, Green, FHV and high-volume FHV separately. Recorded submissions do not establish every actual trip or transport market share. “All services” combines these published records without treating ordinary FHV as a parent of high-volume FHV.
