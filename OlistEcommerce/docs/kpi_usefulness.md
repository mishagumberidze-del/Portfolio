# Why these measures are in the report

The executive page has six headline measures, each attached to a clear marketplace question:

| Question | Measure | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| How much merchandise value is represented by commercial orders? | Sales / GMV | Item-price value excluding canceled/unavailable orders and freight; not profit or recognized net revenue |
| How much commercial order activity produced that value? | Sales Orders | Same commercial population as GMV; the eight commercial orders without items remain explicit in the denominator |
| What is the average commercial order value? | Average Order Value | GMV / commercial orders; distribution and product mix can change the average |
| What share of orders ended canceled? | Cancellation Rate | Canceled / all filtered orders; source status is an observed outcome, not a causal explanation |
| How often were evaluable delivered orders received by the promised date? | On-Time Delivery % | Eligible delivered order weighting; missing actual/promise dates do not count as successful delivery |
| What review experience is recorded? | Average Review Score | Valid recorded reviews; not all purchasers respond, and a 1–5 review scale is not NPS |

Customer frequency/cohorts, product/seller contribution, freight and delivery detail remain on their relevant diagnostic pages. They help explain the headline results and do not all compete as executive KPIs. Factual completed-purchase groups replace unsupported loyalty/risk labels. Cohorts start with commercial purchases and omit partial boundary months.

Comparisons support investigation. They do not establish that delivery causes repeat buying, that low repeat purchase implies churn, or that a seller/category should receive an investment without cost, capacity and other business evidence.
